This under appreciated town in Mozambique's east coast is a gem for backbackers and frequent travellers alike

Kayaking in the Bay

With a 2 600km (1,616 miles) coastline, the second-longest on the African continent after Somalia, Mozambique has a lot of beach to offer, as well as a lot of gorgeous secluded beaches.

Inhambane's Tofo Beach and Vilankulos are destinations that promise a great mix of adventure, natural beauty and history. Whether you're an adventure seeker, a history buff, or simply a beach lover, these coastal treasures will capture your heart and create memories to last a lifetime.

Scuba Diving & Snorkeling

Explore a vibrant underwater world teeming with manta rays, whale sharks, and colorful coral reefs. Tofo is a world-renowned dive destination with warm waters and excellent visibility—perfect for beginners and seasoned divers alike. Manta Reef is one such exciting destination.Some operators will take you to other destinations like Vilankulos and Morrungulo.

Deep Sea Fishing

Try your hand at catching game fish or learn the art of traditional fishing from local guides. Both sport fishing charters and cultural fishing tours are available

Ocean safaris: Swim alongside whale sharks

Imagine slipping into the Indian Ocean beside a whale shark, the world's largest fish, as it glides through the blue water. Tofo's two-hour ocean safaris search for whale sharks, manta rays, dolphins and turtles, with snorkelling opportunities when wildlife and sea conditions allow. Tofo Scuba publishes a price of $55 per person for an ocean safari; allow roughly $55–$80 as a practical budget depending on operator and inclusions. Trips run year-round, but sightings are never guaranteed. Whale sharks can be encountered throughout the year, while October to March is often a particularly attractive period for visitors hoping to see them. Book early in your stay so you have another opportunity if the first trip encounters rough seas or little wildlife

Scuba diving: Explore reefs vibrant with marine life. Tofo is not yet a heavily trafficked destination so expect unspoiled waters.

Tofo is a serious diving destination, not just a beach resort with a dive shop. Local reefs offer encounters with manta rays, reef sharks, turtles, rays, tropical fish and fascinating smaller marine species. Popular sites include Manta Reef, known for manta-ray encounters, and deeper offshore reefs suited to experienced divers. Tofo Scuba lists a standard dive at $55 with rental equipment or $50 with your own gear, a Discover Scuba experience at $110, and a full PADI Open Water certification course at $490. Certified divers should budget around $50–$70 per dive, allowing for possible surcharges and equipment extras. Diving is available throughout the year, but May to October is often favoured for cooler, drier weather and better underwater visibility; bring a thicker wetsuit for winter dives. Many offshore sites have currents and challenging boat entries, so check the conditions and required certification with your operator

Fishing trips: Take on the Indian Ocean

For anglers, Tofo and the wider Inhambane coastline offer opportunities to charter boats and fish the Indian Ocean. Depending on the operator, target species and conditions, trips may focus on offshore sport fishing or more relaxed coastal excursions. Budget approximately $100–$300 or more per boat for a shorter private outing, with full-day offshore charters potentially costing substantially more; these are broad planning estimates rather than verified Tofo-wide tariffs. The best trip depends on seasonal fish movements, sea conditions and whether you are sharing a boat or booking privately.

Best times to visit

  • April–May: A quieter change of season
  • June–September: Whales, diving and outdoor exploration
  • October–March: Warm water and whale sharks
