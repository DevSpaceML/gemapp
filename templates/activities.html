Kayaking in the Bay

With a 2 600km (1,616 miles) coastline, the second-longest on the African continent after Somalia, Mozambique has a lot of beach to offer, as well as a lot of gorgeous secluded beaches.

Inhambane's Tofo Beach and Vilankulos are destinations that promise a great mix of adventure, natural beauty and history. Whether you're an adventure seeker, a history buff, or simply a beach lover, these coastal treasures will capture your heart and create memories to last a lifetime.

Snorkelling at Manta Reef

Scuba Diving & Snorkeling

Explore a vibrant underwater world teeming with manta rays, whale sharks, and colorful coral reefs. Tofo is a world-renowned dive destination with warm waters and excellent visibility—perfect for beginners and seasoned divers alike. Manta Reef is one such exciting destination.Some operators will take you to other destinations like Vilankulos and Morrungulo.

Deep Sea Fishing

Try your hand at catching game fish or learn the art of traditional fishing from local guides. Both sport fishing charters and cultural fishing tours are available

Ocean Safaris

Get up close and personal with the giants of the sea on a thrilling boat adventure. Spot whale sharks year-round and witness humpback whales during their migration (June–October)

Yoga and Wellness

Rejuvenate your mind and body with beachside yoga sessions and holistic treatments. Several lodges offer daily yoga classes, massages, and wellness retreats surrounded by nature.
