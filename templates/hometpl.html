Where Adventure Meets the Ocean

Tofo is Mozambique’s coastal playground, a sun-soaked paradise where turquoise waters, golden beaches, and a vibrant social scene come together to create an unforgettable escape. Whether you’re chasing adventure, looking to unwind, or ready to dance the night away, Tofo offers the perfect blend of laid-back beach living and exhilarating entertainment

Beyond the main beach

Venture beyond Tofo to discover the character and culture of the Inhambane region. Explore nearby coastal villages, visit the historic city of Inhambane, browse local markets, and experience the warmth of Mozambican hospitality. Take a boat trip, arrange a fishing excursion, or seek out quieter beaches where the coastline feels wonderfully untouched. Every excursion is another reason to stay a day longer.

Golden Beaches and Endless Summer Days

Spend your mornings swimming in crystal-clear waters, sunbathing on pristine beaches, or taking long walks along the spectacular coastline. Catch a surf lesson, explore hidden stretches of shoreline, or simply settle beneath a palm tree with a refreshing drink and the sound of the waves in the background.

Experience the Nightlife

When darkness falls, follow the music to Tofo’s bars and beachside hangouts. Enjoy DJ sets, live music, impromptu parties, and dance nights that bring the community together. The atmosphere is refreshingly informal: flip-flops, sandy feet, good music, and great company are all part of the experience. Whether you prefer a quiet drink under the stars or a night of dancing until late, Tofo delivers entertainment without losing its easygoing charm
